Terms & Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

Plain summary: Budget Padmanabham uses Gmail sign-in to identify approved family members. Family financial content is encrypted in the browser with the family privacy password before it is saved. The database can sync encrypted records, but it cannot read the family content without that password.

1. What This App Is For

Budget Padmanabham is a family expense and income tracker. It lets an approved family group record expenses, recurring income, categories, budgets, savings goals, and encrypted analytics for household use.

2. Login And Family Access

The app supports Google/Gmail sign-in through Supabase Auth. A person can create a family, or join an existing one by entering its invite code together with the family privacy password. Both are required: the password is verified by the server, so an invite code on its own does not grant access. Joining is immediate and does not need approval. The family admin can lock or unlock joining, rotate the invite code, and remove members.

Only approved family members should receive the invite code and the family privacy password. Anyone who has both can decrypt family records in the browser.

3. End-To-End Encryption

The family privacy password is used in the browser to derive an encryption key. The app encrypts family content before sending it to Supabase, including family name, member display names, category names, category limits, monthly budget, savings goal, expenses, income, notes, and analytics snapshots.

The family privacy password is not sent to Supabase. If all approved members lose the family privacy password, encrypted family records cannot be recovered by the app owner, database owner, Supabase, Vercel, or Google.

4. What We Collect

The app stores the minimum information needed to operate login, membership, invite review, encrypted syncing, and realtime updates:

  • Google account identity from Supabase Auth, such as email address and profile name.
  • User ids, family ids, row ids, timestamps, invite status, membership role, and removal state.
  • Encrypted text payloads for family content and analytics.
  • Local browser storage for the signed-in session and remembered family encryption key on that device.

5. What We Do Not Collect

The app does not ask for bank logins, card numbers, UPI PINs, account passwords, government IDs, live location, contacts, or the family privacy password.

6. What The Database Owner Can See

The database owner can see authentication metadata and operational metadata such as emails, user ids, family ids, table row ids, timestamps, invite status, membership roles, and encrypted payload text. The database owner should not be able to read encrypted family financial content unless an approved family member shares the family privacy password or decrypted content outside the app.

7. Member Responsibilities

Family members are responsible for entering accurate records, keeping the invite code and family privacy password private, and using this app only for lawful family budgeting. Do not add another person without their permission.

8. Deleting Or Leaving

A family admin can remove family members. If the admin leaves or is removed, ownership can be reassigned according to the app's family rules. Removing a member stops their future app access, but it cannot revoke information they already viewed or copied while approved.

9. Service Providers

The app uses Supabase for authentication, database, and realtime sync, Vercel for hosting, and Google for Google sign-in. These providers may process operational metadata needed to run the app.

10. Changes

These terms and this privacy policy may be updated as the app changes. Continued use after an update means you accept the latest version.

11. Contact

For questions, contact the family admin or app administrator who invited you to use Budget Padmanabham.
